Comprehensive Care & Labor

From heavy lifting to gentle companionship, I handle the tasks that are becoming too difficult to manage alone.

Home Handyman

Keeping the house safe, functional, and well-maintained without the stress of doing it yourself.

  • Minor Repairs: Fixing cabinet doors, patching drywall, and general maintenance.
  • Safety Fixtures: Swapping out hard-to-reach lightbulbs, changing smoke detector batteries safely.
  • Mounting & Assembly: Securing TVs to the wall, assembling IKEA furniture, and hanging heavy pictures.

Patient Tech Support

Technology is confusing. I provide slow, jargon-free help so seniors can stay connected to their family.

  • Device Setup: Getting new iPads, smartphones, and smart TVs working perfectly.
  • Video Calling: Teaching how to use Zoom or FaceTime to see the grandchildren.
  • Troubleshooting: Fixing confusing Wi-Fi issues, forgotten passwords, and explaining online scams.

Heavy Lifting & Moving

Protecting fragile backs. I provide the physical strength needed to move, clear, and rearrange the home.

  • Rearranging Furniture: Moving heavy couches, beds, or wardrobes between rooms.
  • Stair Assistance: Bringing heavy boxes from the basement or attic up and down stairs.
  • Throwing Things Out: Hauling heavy junk, old mattresses, or furniture out to the curb.

Errands & Groceries

When mobility is a challenge or the weather is bad, let me run the town and do the heavy lifting.

  • Heavy Groceries: Buying and delivering cases of water, pet food, or heavy bags right to the kitchen.
  • Pharmacy Runs: Safe, reliable prescription pickups and drop-offs.
  • Post Office: Dropping off packages, returning Amazon boxes, or fetching the mail.

Companionship

Sometimes the most important job isn't fixing a sink, it's sharing a smile and helping with daily routines.

  • Home Cooking: Preparing fresh, warm, healthy meals right in their kitchen.
  • Dog Walking: Energetic walks for furry friends when the owner is unable to.
  • Games & Chats: Always up for a game of Cribbage, Scrabble, or a friendly conversation.

Yard & Snow Care

Keeping the outdoors safe and beautiful, so seniors can enjoy their property without the physical strain.

  • Snow Shoveling: Clearing stairs, walkways, and driveways to prevent slips and ensure safe winter access.
  • Leaf Raking: Bagging up fall leaves, branches, and clearing seasonal yard debris.
  • Light Gardening: Planting flowers, watering the garden, and simple weeding to maintain a lovely yard.

If you live out of town or have a busy schedule, you can hire me to be your reliable set of hands. I offer comprehensive elderly assistance. Yes, I provide reliable seasonal outdoor care. This includes snow shoveling for stairs, walkways, and driveways to prevent slips in the winter, as well as leaf raking and light gardening during the warmer months.
