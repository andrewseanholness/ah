App Quick Guide

Adding Appts & Taxes

Tap + Appt. When you check "Include GST & QST", the app perfectly calculates the exact 5% GST and 9.975% QST for Quebec automatically onto the invoice!

Track Expenses & Gas

Use the new Finances tab to log your gas receipts and hair supplies. We calculate your "Net Profit" by subtracting these expenses from your total hair styling revenue.

One-Tap SMS Invoicing

Tap View Invoice on any appointment. Review the clean receipt and tap Copy SMS Text to instantly text the bill to the client's family.

Smart Reminders

Tap Cal on your home screen to instantly add an appointment to your iPhone Calendar to get 24-hour and 1-hour notifications.

Cloud Saving

Everything saves securely to the cloud and is locked to your device. You will never lose your client notes or tax numbers.
